MY JUKEBOX

A music sharing party game

Each round reveals a themed prompt, and every player secretly picks a song that fits. After rating and guessing, the best song wins the round. By the end you have a playlist that says something about everyone who played.

How a game goes

  1. The prompt

    A theme is revealed to everyone at once. Something like a song that sums up the 1990s.

  2. Everyone picks

    Each player privately finds a song that fits and submits it. No peeking.

  3. Rate and guess

    Score every song but your own out of ten, and guess who chose each one. Both are optional, and you decide before the game starts.

  4. The reveal

    Songs come out one at a time, with the chooser and who guessed right. The highest average wins the round and joins the playlist.

Two ways to play

Round one phone

The whole game runs on a single shared device, passed around the table. Nobody needs an account, and nothing has to be installed.

Everyone on their own

Or open a room and share the code. The host can drive playback so the whole room hears the same thing at the same time, or let everyone listen at their own pace.

Scoring, if you want it

Two switches, both off to begin with, and either works without the other. Rate songs scores each song out of ten and gives the round to the highest average. Guess the chooser asks who picked what, and builds a Best Detective table from who reads the room best. Turn both off and it is just sharing music: a prompt, a song each, and a listen.
